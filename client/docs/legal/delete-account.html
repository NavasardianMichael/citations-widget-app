Delete your account and data

Bible Citations · Last updated 19 September 2026

There are two ways to delete your account. Both remove the same data.

Option 1 · fastest

In the app

Open the app → Profile → Delete account. After you confirm, the account and its data are deleted immediately.

Option 2

By request, without installing the app

Email navasardianmichael@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject Delete my account.

Requests are completed within 30 days and you will receive a confirmation.

What is deleted

Email address, name, password hash, Google account identifier and profile picture, social link and profile settings, saved citations, all widget settings, and every session and access token.

What is kept

Approved citations you submitted remain in the shared collection, no longer attributed to you or linked to you in any way. Say so in your email if you want those removed too.

Anonymous crash reports in Sentry, which do not identify you, expire on Sentry's retention schedule (90 days).
